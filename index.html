How to use in 30 seconds

Everything updates live as you type. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

1

Pick your instrument (Forex, Gold, Crypto, Indices) and enter your account equity.

2

Choose how much you risk per trade (1–2% is common). This is the most you lose if your stop loss is hit.

3

Enter your stop loss distance – how far (in pips/points) price can move against you before you exit.

4

Read the Recommended Broker Order. Type the lot size into your platform. Pick 2–4 layers to split it into several smaller entries.

5

Use the Stepped Compounding Plan tab to map a path from your starting balance to a goal, then click Load Sizer on any step.

The formula
Lots = (Equity × Risk%) ÷ (Stop Loss × Pip Value)

Example: $1,000 × 1.5% = $15 risk. 20 pips × $10 = $200 per lot → 0.07 lots.

🔒 PDPA 2010 & Data Processing Notice

In compliance with the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (Malaysia), this application operates on a Zero-Knowledge Client-Side Architecture. All equity amounts, lot sizes, and parameters are calculated strictly inside your local browser runtime memory (RAM) and are not collected, processed, or stored on external servers. Static assets are served via secure Content Delivery Networks (CDNs) hosted across international edge nodes outside Malaysia.
